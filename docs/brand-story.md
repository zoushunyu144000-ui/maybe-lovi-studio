# MAYBE LOVI — Brand Story

## Brand essence
MAYBE LOVI is not only about aroma or candles. The emotional core is **care made tangible**.

The brand should communicate that a gift can carry:
- love
- thoughtfulness
- sincerity
- care
- quiet companionship

## Name story
The name combines personal relationships and love:
- **May** — the founder's name
- **M** — also connected to Mom / mother
- **be** — connected to her dog, Baby Girl
- **Lovi** — love

This makes the brand name personal rather than purely decorative.

## Founder story — internal summary
May discovered aroma/candle-making during a difficult period in life. She found the process calming and restorative, then enrolled in a course to learn the craft. She is naturally introverted and does not express everything through words, so making things by hand became another way to communicate care.

She already runs her own studio in another service field, and MAYBE LOVI is developing as a new handmade brand alongside that experience.

## Recommended public-facing framing
A suitable public narrative is:

> MAYBE LOVI started from a small wish to find something gentle in everyday life. During a difficult period, May discovered the quiet process of making aroma pieces by hand. What began as a way to slow down and care for herself gradually became something she wanted to share with others. Every piece is made with patience, with the hope that the person receiving it can feel remembered, cared for, and loved.

## Working brand thought
**先照顾好自己，再把这份温柔做成礼物送给别人。**

English working thought:
**A little softness, made to be given.**

These are working directions, not confirmed slogans.

## Tone of voice
- gentle, not sentimental
- warm, not childish
- personal, not overly confessional
- sincere, not salesy
- quiet, not cold
- feminine, but not overly cute

## Avoid
- making the website overly heavy or personal
- generic luxury language with no emotional substance
- overly childish pink/cute treatment
- exaggerated wellness or healing claims
