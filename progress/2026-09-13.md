### Lightweight cart
Current confirmed flow:
`Product exterior -> scent -> quantity -> Add to Cart -> cart review -> WhatsApp order handoff`

Requirements:
- cart line items include product, scent, quantity, price and line total
- same product + same scent may merge quantity
- same product + different scent stays separate
- cart displays subtotal
- no integrated online checkout at this stage
- shipping and availability are confirmed in WhatsApp
- WhatsApp order CTA generates a readable summary from the live cart and opens the merchant chat with the text prefilled
- customer manually presses Send
