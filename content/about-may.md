## Short version
Hi, I'm May.

MAYBE LOVI started from a small wish to bring a little more warmth into everyday life. I discovered aroma-making during a difficult period and slowly fell in love with the quiet process of making things by hand.

I hope every MAYBE LOVI piece feels like a small reminder that someone thought of you, cared about you, and chose something with intention.

## Chinese working version
嗨，我是 May。

MAYBE LOVI 是从一个很简单的念头开始的：想把生活里那些温柔、被关心的感觉，做成一份可以送出去的礼物。

我希望 MAYBE LOVI 的每一件作品，都不只是一个东西，而是一份认真准备过的心意。
