# Visual Notes

## Current observations
- The client is drawn to soft, romantic, gift-oriented candle/aroma visuals.
- The direction should feel personal and warm rather than corporate.
- The existing logo already carries much of the brand personality, so the website should support it rather than compete with it.

## Current risk
The phrase "macaron / Instagram style" is too broad to design from by itself.

The additional websites the client sends should be used to determine:
- how soft vs minimal the final site should be
- how much pink/pastel is appropriate
- whether typography should feel editorial, handwritten, or clean-modern
- how much illustration/decorative detail to use
- whether the overall site should feel more like a gift studio or a lifestyle fragrance brand

## Decision rule
Do not finalize the visual system until at least 3 useful client references have been compared.
